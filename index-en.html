4General requirements

4.1

Impartiality and independence

Manual, section 6; 4.3

Inspection work is impartial. Structure, management and the way staff are paid must not compromise that impartiality. Risks to impartiality are identified on an ongoing basis, analysed and treated, and top management commits to it in writing.

Confidentiality

Manual, section 7

The body is responsible for the information it obtains or creates during inspection. Binding confidentiality undertakings are in place and, where the law requires disclosure, the client is informed.

(Type A)

Manual, section 5.5; 6.4

To be Type A (third party), neither the body nor its inspection personnel may be the designer, manufacturer, supplier, installer, purchaser, owner, user or maintainer of the items they inspect.
